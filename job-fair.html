The Mega Job Fair held on 5th March 2022 at Virudhunagar, Tamil Nadu created meaningful employment opportunities. Leading companies from various sectors participated to recruit talented candidates. The event successfully connected skills with the right career paths.

The Mega Job Fair held on 27th May 2022 at Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu brought together 40+ leading companies. The event offered career opportunities across IT, banking, finance, healthcare, and service sectors. It served as a strong platform for students and job seekers to begin their professional journey..

The Assistlana Mega Job Fair 2022 was conducted at Virudhunagar Hindu Nadars’ Senthikumara Nadar College, Virudhunagar, Tamil Nadu.
