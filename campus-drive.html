Our Recruitment Journey

From signing to offer in 4 transparent steps

1

Signing (MoU)Memorandum of Understanding

The campus drive process begins with signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the college. This agreement establishes a formal partnership to conduct placement drives and create career opportunities for students.

2

Student Orientation

Students are introduced to the campus drive process, eligibility criteria, and recruitment roadmap to ensure clarity and preparedness.

3

Employee Registration & Fixing Date

Eligible students register for the campus drive, and recruitment dates are finalized in coordination with the college.

4

In-Campus Training & Placement Drive

In-Campus Training & Placement Drive Final-stage training and placement activities are conducted on campus to prepare students and facilitate successful recruitment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What is campus placement drive?
A campus placement drive is a recruitment process conducted in collaboration with colleges to provide job opportunities for eligible students.
What is the role of MoU in the campus drive?
The MoU establishes a formal partnership between the college and our organization to ensure a smooth and transparent placement process.
Who can participate in the campus drive?
Students who meet the eligibility criteria specified during the student orientation can participate in the campus drive.
Is training provided before the placement drive?
Yes, in-campus training is conducted to prepare students with required skills before the placement drive.
Will the placement drive be conducted on campus?
Yes, both training and placement activities are conducted within the college campus.
What type of job roles are offered?
Job roles vary based on company requirements and student skill sets.
